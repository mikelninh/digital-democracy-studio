03 · Ownership & management

The hard part starts after identity resolution.

Once we know which physical asset we are talking about, the next question is who owned, managed or controlled it at a specific time.

Nov 2024 / Feb 2025

IMO 9413547 has dated owner/manager records

The Ukrainian portal displays Celestine Navigation as owner, Stream as commercial manager and Sterling as safety manager on dated records.

Dec 2025

EU legal act links the same IMO to Nova’s managed fleet

The EU lists Nova Shipmanagement and Citrine Marine, and refers to Amulet IMO 9413547 and Bobcat IMO 9422457 in that network.

2025 corroboration

ARROW shows a clear Citrine → Nova owner/manager pairing

Ukraine’s GUR portal separately records Citrine as owner and Nova as commercial manager for ARROW, IMO 9413561.

As of 1 Sep 2026

Do not invent a timeless “current owner”

The records describe different dates and changing corporate relationships. A fresh registry/IMO-company check is required before making a current ownership claim.

04 · What the sanctions record proves

Asset identity is strong. The wider network still has boundaries.

Supported

UK sanctions identify the two IMOs under 2024 names. EU legislation later refers to those same IMOs under different names and sanctions Nova/Citrine. The Council describes the targeted shipping companies as part of the shadow-fleet value chain.

Not yet supported

A natural-person UBO behind every vessel-owning company; a single unchanged manager across the full period; or the exact proceeds generated by any one voyage.

05 · Confidence / gap

What should an analyst collect next?

Next move
Freeze the graph “as of today”.

Pull fresh IMO/company-registry ownership and management records for each vessel, preserve the retrieval timestamp, then compare them with the UK 2024 and EU 2025 states. Any change becomes an explicit temporal edge instead of silently overwriting history.

Why this case matters:

Name matching is easy to game and easy to break. Stable identifiers, source dates and separate owner/manager roles are what make asset tracing defensible.
