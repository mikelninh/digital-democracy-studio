02 · Investigative reasoning

Not every lead survives.

The product makes hypotheses and disconfirming evidence visible. Five publications repeating one allegation do not become five independent sources.

SUPPORTED

Mihailo has material indirect ownership

Registry and ownership records create a defensible chain to 36.4% economic / 30.8% voting interest.

REJECTED

Mihailo is the sanctioned Mikhail Petrovich

Name similarity is outweighed by conflicts in DOB, nationality and passport.

UNRESOLVED

The Cedar nominee principal is known

The supplied declaration is redacted. The system stops rather than filling the gap.

WEAKENED / REJECTED

The payment diversion is unauthorised

Assignment notice + supplier verification provide a plausible authorised explanation.

SOURCE LINEAGE

6 publications can still equal 1 evidence origin.

A duplicated or syndicated article is displayed as repetition, not independent corroboration.

RiskWatch allegation → mirror → repost → social citation   =   one underlying origin
03 · How I work

Problem → system → verified outcome.

I used the agent-heavy workflow described in the “Product Architect” idea as an operating model, not as a title. My job was to make the important human decisions explicit, let agents accelerate implementation and review, and then verify the product itself.

01 · OUTCOME

Define the analyst decision

Who owns, what is risky, what is actually proven, and what should be acquired next?

02 · CONSTRAINTS

Set evidence boundaries

Name ≠ identity. Operation ≠ ownership. Missing ≠ zero. Consequential claims need evidence.

03 · ARCHITECTURE

Design the system around those rules

Evidence vault, entity resolution, claim ledger, ownership/control graph and explicit uncertainty.

04 · AGENTS

Delegate implementation and review

Separate implementation, regression, adversarial review and browser-QA passes.

05 · VERIFY

Use the working product

Real browser tests found runtime and interaction bugs that passing unit tests missed.

06 · MEASURE

Score the analytical result

Hidden gold answer, critical-fail caps and an evidence-budget challenge: 95 → 100.
