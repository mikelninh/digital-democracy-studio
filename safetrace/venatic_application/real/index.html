01 · Conclusion

What can we actually prove?

Three separate questions. Three different evidentiary answers.

Advocacy documentedYes.

PFG publicly disclosed that it wanted the planned 1 Aug 2025 obligation delayed until a practical revision.

Requested delay enactedYes.

The first amendment was promulgated in July 2025 and extended implementation by seven months to 1 Mar 2026.

Lobbying caused itNot proven.

The bill’s executive footprint says interest representatives did not materially contribute to its content.

Analyst conclusionA meat-industry company asked for a delay, and a delay was later enacted. That is real policy alignment. The available public record does not establish that PFG, VDF or “the meat lobby” caused the legislature to make that choice.
02 · Reason

Follow the evidence through time.

The date matters. A proposal, an enacted law and today’s law are different records.

28 Jun 2024

PFG discloses its lobbying objective

The planned 1 Aug 2025 obligation should not take effect before a practical revision of the law.

3 Jun 2025

A bill proposes the delay

Bundestag printed paper 21/327 proposes moving the transition deadline from 1 Aug 2025 to 1 Mar 2026.

22 Jul 2025

The change is actually enacted

The official procedure record shows the first amendment was promulgated; the enacted measure extended implementation by seven months to 1 Mar 2026.

Official account

The causal claim stops here

The same legislative materials say interest representatives did not materially contribute to the bill and also point to Länder ministers and implementation concerns.

1 Sep 2026

The law has changed again

The current consolidated §40(2) now uses 1 Jan 2027. SafeTrace keeps the 2025 finding temporal rather than presenting an old deadline as current law.

20 Jul 2026

PFG continues lobbying the next amendment

A new exact Lobbyregister record sets out objectives on downgrading and imported goods.

This is why “source + date + version” matters: a true historical finding can still be wrong if presented as the current state.

05 · Next move

What would an analyst investigate next?

The unresolved question is causal: what happened between stakeholder advocacy and the final policy decision?

Highest-value records

Ministerial meeting calendars, consultation files, departmental correspondence, committee hearing material and version history of the draft could show whether specific arguments or wording moved through the process.

Test competing explanations

The official bill points to the Länder agriculture ministers and implementation problems. Other industry, organic, animal-welfare and food-service groups also advocated on the same law. A credible investigation must test them too.
