# SafeTrace v1.0 — first bounded portfolio

## Why a bounded portfolio

The audit found 38 records at 24 points or higher. SafeTrace cannot responsibly run 38 investigations at once.

The first operating rule is therefore:

- maximum 5 active cases;
- maximum 10 additional scoped cases;
- all other records remain visible in the Radar.

Selection considers urgency, source access, achievable evidence improvement, variety of case type and representation across the first four seasons.

---

## Active portfolio — five cases

### 1. CASE-015 — Das Rätsel der zehn Kontrollen

**Why active:** fastest measurable accountability test.

**Case type:** official data-quality correction.

**First deliverable:** documented correction request to LAGeSo covering the 26-versus-36 discrepancy and the total count.

**Target actor:** LAGeSo Berlin.

**Initial impact level:** Evidence Impact.

**First measurable result:** answer received, number corrected or discrepancy explained.

**Stop condition:** close as corrected when the public page and explanation are consistent; escalate only if a material contradiction remains.

---

### 2. CASE-012 — Die unsichtbaren Toten

**Why active:** national data gap with strong animal-welfare relevance and a reproducible federal-state request.

**Case type:** systemic data gap.

**First deliverable:** one standardised data request and a 16-state response matrix.

**Target actors:** competent federal body and the 16 Länder.

**Initial impact level:** Evidence Impact moving toward Accountability Impact.

**First measurable result:** number of responses, datasets identified and definitions reconciled.

**Stop condition:** after the first complete response cycle, publish the data map and decide whether a second collection round is justified.

---

### 3. CAND-033 — 181 Fälle – und trotzdem keine Statistik

**Why active:** highest-need child-protection case with a verified starting data gap.

**Case type:** child-protection reporting gap.

**First deliverable:** source and jurisdiction map covering reporting, recognition, outcome and preventive follow-up.

**Target actors:** relevant federal bodies, accident insurers and Länder.

**Initial impact level:** Evidence Impact.

**First measurable result:** institutions able to report compatible case and outcome fields.

**Stop condition:** pause publication claims beyond the verified federal gap until definitions and jurisdiction are mapped.

---

### 4. CAND-039 — Die fehlenden Schutzplätze, die niemand beziffern kann

**Why active:** immediate safety relevance and a clear capacity-versus-demand question.

**Case type:** service capacity and unmet demand.

**First deliverable:** regional women’s shelter capacity, rejection and accessibility data model plus pilot collection.

**Target actors:** Länder, municipalities and responsible federal coordination bodies.

**Initial impact level:** Evidence and Accountability Impact.

**First measurable result:** regions with capacity, rejection and accessibility data; identified data gaps and responsible bodies.

**Stop condition:** do not estimate national unmet demand from incomplete regional samples; publish coverage limitations visibly.

---

### 5. CAND-045 — 474.700 Menschen – und die Kosten kennt niemand zentral

**Why active:** large affected population, fragmented responsibility and a measurable housing-outcome question.

**Case type:** cross-jurisdiction service and outcome gap.

**First deliverable:** municipal pilot dataset for cost, duration and exits to permanent housing.

**Target actors:** selected municipalities, Länder and federal homelessness coordination.

**Initial impact level:** Evidence Impact moving toward System Impact.

**First measurable result:** municipalities able to report comparable cost, duration and housing-exit fields.

**Stop condition:** do not rank municipalities before definitions, case mix and data quality are comparable.

A scoped case needs:

- exact jurisdiction;
- target actor;
- source diversity plan;
- strongest alternative explanation;
- one 14-day deliverable;
- preliminary impact level;
- reason it should displace an active case, if applicable.

## Radar

All remaining records stay public and searchable. Radar status is valuable: it preserves leads without pretending capacity exists.

Radar cases may be promoted when:

- a new official document materially improves evidence;
- a deadline or consultation creates urgency;
- a citizen or reviewer contributes a decisive public source;
- an active case closes;
- the portfolio review documents a stronger public-value case.

---

## Weekly portfolio review

Every active case receives one status:

- on track;
- blocked by missing source;
- waiting for response;
- review required;
- ready to publish;
- close or refer.

The portfolio review records:

- completed deliverable;
- next deliverable;
- owner;
- deadline;
- new evidence;
- changed risks;
- impact status;
- continue, pause, close or replace decision.

## First product success test

The portfolio model succeeds when:

- no more than five cases are active;
- every active case has a visible next deliverable;
- a reader can identify the target actor and first measurable result;
- at least one case closes after a correction or adequate explanation;
- at least one case receives a substantive official response;
- no Radar score is presented as proof of wrongdoing.
