# SafeTrace v1.0 — Spuren im System

## Product promise

A citizen should be able to turn a public-source observation into a fair, checkable and actionable case without turning a high score into an accusation.

SafeTrace v1.0 must answer five questions on every case page:

1. **Why does this matter now?**
2. **What is verified, and what is not?**
3. **Who can realistically change something?**
4. **What exactly happens next?**
5. **How will we know whether anything improved?**

v1.0 is not a platform for anonymous accusations, leaked documents or automated guilt decisions. Public-source investigation remains the production scope. Sensitive evidence stays disabled until a qualified partner, security review and legal process exist.

---

## What we learned from the current 39 records

The current system is strong at discovery:

- every record has a research question;
- every record cites at least one public source;
- every record proposes a first action or sprint;
- opened cases clearly separate verified claims from unknowns;
- the seven opened Tierwohl cases already contain possible impact metrics and win-win-win paths.

The current system is not yet an operating portfolio:

- almost every candidate receives a high priority score, so priority is compressed;
- a high score currently mixes importance with feasibility;
- no record names an accountable action owner;
- no record defines both a deadline and a stop condition;
- candidate cards do not yet contain explicit unknowns, counterevidence or publication gates;
- impact metrics exist for opened cases, but baseline, target, source, owner and cadence are not yet bound into an operational contract;
- there is no capacity rule preventing dozens of high-scoring cases from becoming simultaneous active work.

The central v1.0 correction is therefore:

> **Separate importance, feasibility, publication readiness and impact readiness.**

## The v1.0 information architecture

### 1. Home — What is being missed?

The landing page should show:

- four current seasons;
- a topic-neutral case search;
- current public metrics;
- the five most urgent active investigations;
- official responses, corrections and newly opened data;
- a clear `Start a public-source case` entry point;
- a visible statement that priority is not guilt.

The most important public metrics are not page views. They are:

- official answers received;
- data sets opened;
- factual corrections made;
- actions assigned to responsible institutions;
- policy or practice changes;
- independently verified outcome changes.

### 2. Radar — candidates, not accusations

Every candidate card shows four separate states:

- **Need:** potential harm, scale, urgency and neglected public value;
- **Feasibility:** source access, falsifiability, jurisdiction clarity, safety and available capacity;
- **Evidence state:** lead, verified starting fact, conflict, data gap or live policy process;
- **Decision:** watch, scope, accept, refer or decline.

The Radar may contain many cases. The active pipeline may not.

### 3. Public case board

Lifecycle lanes:

1. Radar
2. Scoping
3. Accepted
4. Active data sprint
5. Evidence review
6. Right of reply
7. Publishable / closed / refuted
8. Action tracking
9. Impact verified or archived

A case cannot skip required gates.

### 4. Case page

Each case page should have:

#### Story header

- narrative title;
- factual research question;
- current bounded verdict;
- last verified date;
- affected group and jurisdiction.

#### Four independent indicators

- Need
- Feasibility
- Publication readiness
- Impact readiness

No single combined score should imply guilt or truth.

#### Evidence ledger

Every public claim links to:

- a source receipt;
- exact excerpt or structured fact;
- publication date and access date;
- source type;
- confidence and limitations;
- later corrections or superseding documents.

#### Fairness panel

- strongest reasonable counterposition;
- alternative explanations;
- facts that would weaken the case;
- right-of-reply status;
- conflicts of interest for reviewers;
- potential harms caused by publication.

#### Action centre

- responsible institution or actor;
- exact change or answer requested;
- named internal owner;
- deadline;
- deliverable;
- stop / pause / escalation condition;
- safe citizen actions.

#### Impact contract

- baseline;
- desired target or directional test;
- metric definition;
- official or reproducible data source;
- measurement cadence;
- responsible owner;
- 30 / 90 / 365-day checkpoints;
- attribution statement.

#### Corrections and response log

Corrections, replies and verdict changes remain visible and versioned.

### 5. Start a case wizard

TRACE becomes a guided flow:

- **T — Test:** one falsifiable question and affected group;
- **R — Register:** at least one public primary source;
- **A — Assemble:** verified lead, unknowns and alternative explanations;
- **C — Challenge:** other party, safety, privacy and possible harm;
- **E — Evaluate:** need, feasibility, first deliverable and impact hypothesis.

The wizard must reject or redirect:

- private addresses;
- medical records;
- passwords and credentials;
- vulnerable source identities;
- covertly obtained material;
- doxxing, hacking, impersonation or harassment requests.

## The v1.0 decision model

### A. Need score — 0 to 30

This answers: **How important is the question?**

- potential severity;
- number of affected people or animals;
- urgency;
- inequality or vulnerability;
- public value;
- overlookedness.

### B. Feasibility score — 0 to 25

This answers: **Can SafeTrace investigate it fairly now?**

- primary-source access;
- falsifiable question;
- clear jurisdiction and responsible actor;
- lawful and safe method;
- team capacity.

### C. Impact-readiness score — 0 to 25

This answers: **Can knowledge realistically become measurable change?**

- responsible actor identified;
- intervention or request specified;
- baseline available;
- target and metric defined;
- source and cadence available.

### D. Publication gates — pass / incomplete / block

This is not a popularity score.

Required gates:

- material claims supported by source receipts;
- explicit unknowns and strongest counterevidence;
- headline no stronger than evidence;
- editorial review;
- domain review where needed;
- legal/privacy review where needed;
- right of reply where an identifiable party may be harmed;
- correction route and impact-risk assessment.

### Capacity rule

v1.0 permits at most:

- **5 active investigations**;
- **10 scoped cases**;
- unlimited visible Radar candidates.

New cases can displace active work only through a documented priority and urgency decision.

## Verdict taxonomy

Every case must use one bounded verdict type:

- verified factual finding;
- data-quality problem;
- systemic data gap;
- implementation gap;
- policy risk or unresolved trade-off;
- documented network or pattern;
- insufficient evidence;
- claim weakened or refuted;
- corrected and closed;
- monitored live process.

`Scandal`, `corrupt` and `guilty` are never system verdicts without an authoritative legal or evidentiary basis that specifically supports those terms.

## Impact model

### Level 0 — Reach

Views, shares, subscribers and citations. Useful, but not impact on victims, citizens or animals.

### Level 1 — Evidence impact

- incorrect number corrected;
- official answer received;
- source or dataset released;
- definition clarified;
- previously hidden status made public.

### Level 2 — Accountability impact

- responsible actor identified;
- deadline or public commitment created;
- hearing, review or audit initiated;
- formal response published.

### Level 3 — System impact

- policy, budget, procedure or platform rule changed;
- monitoring or enforcement practice altered;
- service capacity increased;
- data collection institutionalised.

### Level 4 — Outcome impact

- fewer injuries, deaths, rejections or repeat violations;
- shorter waiting times;
- improved access, safety, stability or wellbeing;
- reduced disparity between affected groups or regions.

SafeTrace may claim contribution only when the chain of evidence is documented. Causation requires stronger evidence than temporal sequence or public attention.

## Citizen roles

### Reader

Understand the case in five minutes and distinguish facts, unknowns, verdict and priority.

### Proposer

Submit a structured public-source lead and understand why it was accepted, scoped, referred or declined.

### Source checker

Verify official documents, dates, definitions and superseding versions.

### Local data contributor

Provide public municipal or state-level data without submitting private victim information.

### Domain reviewer

Challenge definitions, metrics and practical consequences.

### Responding party

See the exact claim, sources and questions; submit a response or correction that is displayed fairly.

### Editor / moderator

Control publication, corrections, complaints, conflicts and work-in-progress limits.

## What must be true before v1.0

### Product and data

- [ ] all 39 current records migrated to one Case Contract schema;
- [ ] Need, Feasibility, Publication and Impact readiness shown separately;
- [ ] every accepted case has an owner, deliverable, deadline and stop condition;
- [ ] every accepted case has explicit unknowns and counterevidence;
- [ ] every active case identifies the responsible institution and exact ask;
- [ ] impact contract complete or visibly marked `not measurable yet`;
- [ ] maximum five active cases enforced;
- [ ] source receipts and correction history visible.

### Evidence and fairness

- [ ] 100% of published material claims linked to retained source receipts;
- [ ] source snapshots and SHA-256 receipts for all published claims;
- [ ] right-of-reply workflow tested end to end;
- [ ] one case deliberately closed or weakened after counterevidence;
- [ ] public correction and appeal workflow tested;
- [ ] independent editorial, domain and legal/privacy reviewers identified.

### Citizen usability

- [ ] at least 20 external citizen tests;
- [ ] at least 80% correctly distinguish priority, verdict and guilt;
- [ ] at least 90% can identify the next action and responsible actor;
- [ ] five citizen proposals triaged with written reasons;
- [ ] language tested for plain German and mobile use;
- [ ] accessibility review against WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard and screen-reader testing.

### End-to-end proof

- [ ] at least three cases complete the full lifecycle;
- [ ] at least one official correction or substantive response;
- [ ] at least one new dataset, implementation commitment or formal review;
- [ ] public 30/90-day impact note;
- [ ] no unsupported allegation presented as fact;
- [ ] confidential intake and automatic publication remain disabled.

### Operations

- [ ] moderation owner and backup named;
- [ ] correction acknowledgement target: 48 hours;
- [ ] correction decision target: 14 days;
- [ ] case triage target: 7 days;
- [ ] monthly portfolio review and closure decisions;
- [ ] incident response and protective-hold procedure exercised;
- [ ] transparent public changelog.

## Suggested path from v0.3 to v1.0

### v0.4 — Portfolio truth

- run the cross-case audit;
- separate four readiness dimensions;
- add the Case Contract;
- enforce WIP limits.

### v0.5 — Action centre

- owner, target actor, ask, deliverable, deadline and stop condition;
- 30-day action board;
- citizen-safe actions.

### v0.6 — Impact contracts

- baseline, target, metric, data source, cadence and attribution;
- separate reach from evidence, system and outcome impact.

### v0.7 — Evidence and reply

- source receipts and retained originals;
- counterevidence ledger;
- right-of-reply UI;
- visible correction history.

### v0.8 — Citizen beta

- twenty testers;
- five submitted cases;
- moderation and appeal exercise;
- accessibility and comprehension improvements.

### v0.9 — Independent release review

- editorial, domain, legal/privacy and accessibility findings;
- three end-to-end cases;
- one closed or refuted case;
- public release candidate report.

### v1.0 — Public-source civic investigation platform

Release only when the checklist above is evidenced, not merely checked in code.

## v1.0 north-star metric

> **Percentage of accepted cases for which a citizen can identify the verified fact, unresolved question, responsible actor, next action and measurable progress without mistaking priority for guilt.**

Supporting product targets:

- 90% next-action comprehension;
- 80% priority-versus-verdict comprehension;
- 100% published claim-to-source traceability;
- 100% accepted cases with action owner and stop condition;
- 100% active cases with impact contract or explicit `not measurable yet` state.
