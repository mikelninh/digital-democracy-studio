How it works

From a claim to a defensible next move.

SafeTrace is deliberately question-first. The goal is not to make a story sound certain. The goal is to make the evidence boundary visible.

01

Ask the exact question

Define what would count as an answer before collecting convenient fragments.

02

Build the source trail

Keep publisher, authority, date, entity identity and original evidence attached.

03

Separate evidence from inference

Facts, allegations, analytical indicators and unresolved gaps remain visibly different.

04

Move the case forward

Request evidence, compare records, escalate review, correct the case or publish the next justified step.

Trust by design

Useful without pretending certainty.

SafeTrace should make careful investigation easier — not make unsupported conclusions easier to generate.

Non-negotiables

What the system protects

  • ✓Primary-source provenance stays attached to material claims.
  • ✓Unresolved gaps remain visible instead of being smoothed away.
  • ✓Accusations are never generated automatically from weak signals.
  • ✓Corrections, counter-evidence and right of reply remain first-class.
  • ✓Consequential publication and restricted-data use remain human-authorised.
Current boundary

Public-source alpha, not a truth machine.

SafeTrace is designed for public and synthetic evaluation. Restricted partner data, real victim data and consequential publication require explicit authority, privacy/security controls and qualified review. A green test can prove software behaviour; it cannot turn an unresolved analytical claim into a fact.
