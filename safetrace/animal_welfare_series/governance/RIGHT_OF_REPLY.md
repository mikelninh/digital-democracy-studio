# Standard right-of-reply process

## When required

Right of reply is required before publishing a sensitive claim that could materially affect the reputation, legal position or public trust of an identifiable person or organisation.

It is normally optional for a neutral description of already-published official data when no new adverse inference is added, but a correction check is still encouraged.

## Message structure

1. Identify SafeTrace and the planned publication.
2. State the exact factual propositions being checked.
3. Link or attach the public sources relied on.
4. Separate verified facts from unresolved questions.
5. Ask no more than five focused questions.
6. State the proposed publication line.
7. Provide a clear response date and contact route.
8. Explain that a response may be quoted fairly and that later corrections remain possible.

## Project timing standard

- normal case: target five working days;
- time-sensitive public proceeding: target two working days;
- complex technical or historic case: longer where reasonable.

These are editorial standards, not statements of a universal legal deadline.

## Response handling

- preserve the full response and receipt metadata;
- quote accurately and in context;
- distinguish fact corrections from opinion;
- update the claim ledger before publication;
- record no response without implying guilt;
- offer correction after publication.

## Hold rule

A sensitive publication remains blocked when:

- the questions were not successfully delivered;
- a material factual dispute cannot be checked;
- the response introduces credible new evidence;
- independent editorial or legal review is incomplete.
