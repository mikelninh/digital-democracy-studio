# Independent review protocol

## Purpose

Every public episode must survive three distinct reviews. One person may not approve all three tracks. Reviewers disclose relevant employment, funding, activism, client, family or organisational conflicts.

## 1. Editorial review

The reviewer checks:

- the headline does not overstate the body;
- every consequential sentence maps to an exact source or is clearly marked as interpretation;
- counterevidence and the strongest alternative explanation are visible;
- dates, units, denominators and comparisons are understandable;
- the verdict is narrower than or equal to the evidence;
- unanswered questions are not written as insinuations;
- corrections and changes can be understood later.

Decision: `pass`, `pass_with_changes`, or `block`.

## 2. Animal-welfare domain review

The reviewer checks:

- species, life stage, husbandry, transport, slaughter or experimental context is described correctly;
- welfare indicators are not substituted for one another;
- animal counts, events and rates are separated;
- legal minimums are not described as best practice;
- plausible implementation constraints and unintended harms are considered;
- the proposed solution has a credible path to better animal outcomes;
- animal-outcome impact is not inferred from attention or policy activity alone.

Decision: `pass`, `pass_with_changes`, or `block`.

## 3. Legal and privacy review

This is a project checklist, not legal advice. The qualified reviewer checks:

- lawful purpose and public-interest rationale;
- data minimisation and necessity;
- whether individuals need to be named;
- distinction between allegation, administrative finding, court finding and SafeTrace analysis;
- defamation, privacy, copyright, confidentiality and security risks;
- right-of-reply scope and response time;
- whether publication could endanger people, animals, investigations or evidence;
- redactions and retention periods;
- whether a regulator, authority or professional partner is the proper destination.

Decision: `pass`, `pass_with_changes`, or `block`.

## Review record

Each review records:

- reviewer name and role;
- date and artifact commit SHA;
- declared conflicts;
- findings with severity;
- required edits;
- final decision;
- signature or attributable account.

A review cannot be marked complete without a real reviewer identity.
