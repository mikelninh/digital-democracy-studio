# Sensitive-tip partner gate

## Current rule

SafeTrace does not accept confidential evidence, whistleblower identities, private messages, unpublished veterinary records or covertly obtained material.

## A partner must provide

- a named legal entity and accountable lead;
- secure, access-controlled intake;
- encryption and retention policy;
- source-protection and informed-consent procedure;
- qualified editorial and legal review;
- safeguarding and emergency escalation;
- conflict-of-interest policy;
- incident response and breach notification;
- ability to refer animal-welfare emergencies to competent authorities;
- documented deletion and correction process.

## Acceptance test

Before any partner route is shown publicly, SafeTrace must verify:

1. who receives the material;
2. what data are collected and why;
3. where data are stored;
4. who can access them;
5. how urgent animal or human risk is handled;
6. how the source can withdraw or correct;
7. what SafeTrace itself can and cannot see;
8. independent security and legal review.

## What we can do now

- publish partner criteria;
- keep sensitive intake disabled;
- prepare outreach and due-diligence questions;
- direct immediate danger to competent local authorities.

## What remains missing

A named, qualified and independently reviewed partner. This must not be fabricated or implied.
