Fail-closed behaviour

Ambiguous identity

Propagation stops. A fuzzy name match never becomes an upstream owner just because the graph would look neat.

Missing intermediate percentage

Downstream UBO calculation stops at that edge. Missing is not zero and not “probably proportional”.

Circular ownership

The operating principle

Every edge answers “what source establishes this relationship?” Every aggregate answers “which paths produced this number?” Every gap answers “what exact evidence should we collect next?”

Human review required. Risk indicators are not allegations. Analytical UBO candidates are jurisdiction- and rule-scoped, not final legal determinations.
