# Right-of-reply and verification questions

**Do not send automatically.** Confirm current contacts, provide the relevant draft passage, set a reasonable deadline and publish responses fairly.

## Case 006 — DVAG / BDV / parties / BMF

### DVAG

1. Did DVAG contribute to, commission, approve or coordinate BDV’s positions on the Altersvorsorgereformgesetz?
2. What public-policy purpose did DVAG associate with the five donations totalling EUR 340,000?
3. Were any meetings, calls or written exchanges concerning the reform held with recipient parties, MPs, parliamentary staff or BMF between 1 December 2025 and 27 March 2026?
4. Does DVAG dispute the finding that the final law retained full-period cost spreading and tightened the standard-product cost cap?

### BDV

1. Which member organisations contributed to the December 2025 and March 2026 submissions?
2. Which exact amendments did BDV request, and which does it consider adopted, partly adopted or rejected?
3. Please identify all official meetings or written exchanges concerning the reform.

### Recipient parties and BMF

1. Did the party or ministry discuss the reform with DVAG or BDV during the relevant period?
2. Were the donations known to the participants at the time of any such contacts?
3. What safeguards separated party financing from legislative work?
4. Please correct the demand-to-law matrix where it is incomplete or inaccurate.

## Case 007 — Amazon / BMWE / other stakeholders

### Amazon

1. Which exact provisions did Amazon seek on direct-award thresholds and online marketplaces?
2. What meetings or written exchanges occurred with BMWK/BMWE or other federal bodies?
3. Does Amazon consider any provision of the 2026 draft to implement its marketplace-specific request? Please identify the provision.

### BMWE

1. Which organisations were consulted during preparation of the 2026 UVgO draft?
2. What evidence and administrative analysis supported the default direct-award threshold?
3. Does the draft contain a rule specifically enabling direct awards through online marketplaces? If so, where and why?
4. Will submissions and an executive-footprint-style drafting record be published after consultation?
