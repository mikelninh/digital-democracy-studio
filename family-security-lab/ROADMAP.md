- [ ] select five to seven regions through transparent criteria;

- [ ] produce regional profiles without a moral ranking;

## v0.5 — Public beta

- [ ] interactive indicator explorer;
- [ ] policy comparison and cost fields;
- [ ] evidence and counterevidence ledger;
- [ ] plain-language German;
- [ ] accessible mobile and keyboard experience;
- [ ] at least 20 citizen comprehension tests;
- [ ] at least five expert or practitioner reviews.

## v1.0 — Family Security Observatory

Release requires:

- [ ] all seven dimensions have usable baselines or visible `not measurable yet` states;
- [ ] at least one independently evaluated pilot;
- [ ] one intervention weakened or rejected by evidence;
- [ ] public 30/90/365-day impact reporting;
- [ ] official or institutional responses documented;
- [ ] corrections and methodology changes versioned;
- [ ] no unsupported fertility-effect claim;
- [ ] no sensitive public intake.

## Later direction

A future version may become a network of local Family Security Labs sharing open schemas and evaluation protocols. Federation remains blocked until the standalone project demonstrates reliable operations, independent review and honest publication of unsuccessful results.
