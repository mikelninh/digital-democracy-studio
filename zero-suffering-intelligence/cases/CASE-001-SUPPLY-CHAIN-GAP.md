# Case #001 — Farm → slaughterhouse evidence boundary

Updated: 2026-08-13

## Question

Can Zero Suffering Intelligence prove that a named Tönnies Livestock partner farm supplied a specific PFG/Tönnies slaughterhouse?

## What is proven

- Tönnies Livestock publicly profiles **Betrieb Mucke** as a pig-finishing farm in Münsterland and describes a long-term relationship that includes examples such as offtake contracts.
- Tönnies Livestock publicly profiles **Betrieb Mertens** in Sauerland as a partner involved in piglet rearing / pig finishing.
- Tönnies Livestock publicly describes its role in marketing piglets, pigs and slaughter sows across multiple target markets.
- PFG/Tönnies operates documented slaughter infrastructure including Rheda-Wiedenbrück and Weißenfels; the Bundeskartellamt also identified existing Tönnies slaughter operations in its 2025 merger-control record.

## What is still not proven

There is currently no public primary source in the ZSI corpus that ties either named farm to a specific slaughter destination, shipment date or batch.

Therefore ZSI does **not** assert:

`Betrieb Mucke → Rheda-Wiedenbrück`

or

`Betrieb Mertens → Rheda-Wiedenbrück`

Geographic proximity, group membership and a commercial relationship with Tönnies Livestock are insufficient evidence for that edge.

## Additional research lead

Public reporting about the Mertens farm in Helden gives useful identity and husbandry context and describes pigs going to slaughter after finishing, but it does not identify the destination slaughterhouse. It is therefore a research lead, not the missing destination receipt.

## Sources / records still worth pursuing

1. shipment / transport documents that name origin and destination;
2. QS, Initiative Tierwohl or 5xD programme records if they expose the relevant farm-to-facility relationship;
3. veterinary / food-chain information records where lawfully public;
4. supplier audit records;
5. authority, court or administrative records that describe animal flows;
6. a direct confirmation from the farm, Tönnies Livestock or the destination facility that can be independently documented.

## Publication rule

Until one of those source classes supplies an explicit destination, the graph edge stays **dashed / open** as `ZSI-G-0001`.

This is not a failure to complete the drawing. It is the current evidence boundary.
