# Zero Suffering Intelligence — Right of Reply

Updated: 2026-08-13

Right of Reply is part of the evidence workflow. For consequential organisation profiles we preserve a clear route to identify factual errors, provide newer primary sources, add context, contest an interpretation, and request a correction.

## Workflow

1. Freeze the claim bundle being reviewed.
2. Record the official contact route and date checked.
3. Send a concise request linking to exact claim IDs, sources and boundaries.
4. Record the request date and response status.
5. Review factual corrections against source evidence.
6. If a claim changes, create a correction or supersession record rather than silently rewriting history.
7. Publish a neutral response state: `not_requested_yet`, `awaiting_reply`, `replied`, `declined`, or `no_reply_by_deadline`.

## Fairness rules

- No pay-to-remove facts.
- A company response is evidence to review, not automatic truth.
- Lack of response is not evidence of wrongdoing or an admission.
- Material disputes can remain visible with their supporting evidence.
- Prefer corporate communications or press routes over personal contact details.

The operational registry is `right-of-reply.json`. The first three requests are currently **drafted, not sent**. Sending is a separate human action.
