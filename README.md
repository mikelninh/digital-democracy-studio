# Digital Democracy Studio 🔎

**Open, evidence-grounded tools for understanding rights, money, influence and public power.**

Digital Democracy Studio is an independent public-interest research and product studio. It explores how evidence, software and careful human judgement can make public systems more understandable, accountable and useful.

One of the studio's strongest projects is **SafeTrace**: a question-first investigation workflow that keeps evidence, inference, uncertainty and the next research move separate.

## SafeTrace in 60 seconds

The reviewer does not start with a generated conclusion. They inspect the question, evidence boundary and unresolved gap.

```text
question
   ↓
current assessment
   ↓
original evidence
   ↓
what remains unresolved
   ↓
best next research move
```

SafeTrace is designed to preserve:

- original-source provenance
- evidence vs. inference
- entity identity across messy records
- time-bounded ownership and control
- contradictory records
- unresolved gaps
- explicit next research moves
- human analytical authority

The goal is not to generate accusations. It is to make **what is known, what is inferred and what is still missing** inspectable.

## SafeTrace boundary

SafeTrace is **not an accusation engine** and is not authorised for restricted partner data or real victim data by default.

It distinguishes:

- verified facts
- official or court-established findings
- allegations
- analytical indicators
- unresolved gaps
- corrections

Real restricted deployment remains gated on partner authority, privacy/security controls, legal/editorial review and deployment-specific acceptance.
